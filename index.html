Café & Restaurant Customer Discovery Survey

Understanding the real problems facing hospitality businesses

About this research

I'm speaking with café and restaurant owners and managers to understand where time, money and effort are being wasted in their businesses.

I'm not trying to sell you anything. At this stage I'm trying to identify recurring problems that are genuinely worth solving. Responses are confidential and will be compared with feedback from other hospitality businesses to identify common problems and possible opportunities for better systems, automation or AI.

Business name is required so responses can be tracked by business.
